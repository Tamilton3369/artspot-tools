Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

What this app is

Art Spot (“Art Spot Tools”) is a private, in-house application operated by the ART SPOT / Under100 Art Show team. It is not available to the general public. Only accounts explicitly authorized by the operator may grant it access, and its use is limited to running art-show operations: artist payouts, inventory management, show scheduling, and team communications.

What data we access

When you authorize Art Spot with your Google account, it may access:

  • Google Sheets, Drive and Workspace documents — to read, update, and organize artist rosters, sales exports, inventory sheets, and payout workbooks.
  • Gmail — to search, read, and send email related to show operations (for example, sending payout documents and inventory files).
  • Google Calendar — to view and manage show and deadline scheduling.
  • Contacts (read-only) — to resolve team and artist email addresses.

We access only the data of the account(s) that authorize the app, and only the scopes listed above. We do not request or use data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than the operations described here.

What we do with the data

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • Data is used strictly within the ART SPOT / Under100 Art Show business operations listed above.
  • OAuth tokens and working files are stored locally on the operator's machine and are never disclosed to anyone outside the team.

How to revoke access

You can revoke Art Spot's access to your Google account at any time by visiting myaccount.google.com/permissions and removing the app. Revoking immediately disables all access.
